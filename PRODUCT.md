## Platform

web (the product itself is an Android keyboard; this record covers its public website)

## Users

People who type on Gboard every day and want the same feel without Google: ordinary phone users in Slovakia, Czechia, Germany and Spanish-speaking countries who care about privacy but are not technical. A secondary audience is the cypherpunk / nostr / bitcoin crowd that already uses Zapstore; the site speaks to them in the details, not the headline.

## Product Purpose

Droneboard is an open-source Android keyboard that works fully offline (no INTERNET permission) and feels like Gboard. The website exists to make a Gboard user trust it enough to install it and switch. Success: a visitor understands "it's Gboard-like, it never phones home" within seconds and installs it.

## Positioning

The only keyboard that combines Gboard-level swipe in several languages at once (FUTO Swipe encoder with Droneboard's own decoder), next-word prediction from a small neural network that runs on the phone, and offline dictation through Offline Speech to Text including Bluetooth / LE Audio / hearing-aid microphones — with no network permission at all. Built AI-assisted in the open; anyone may take its code.

## Operating Context

- Installed from Zapstore (preferred route), or as an APK from GitHub releases, or tracked with Obtainium (GitHub releases source).
- Dictation requires Offline Speech to Text (also on Zapstore).
- The site is a static multi-language site (EN, SK, CS, DE, ES) generated by website/build.py, hosted at https://cypherpunk.today/droneboard/.
- The site shows the newest posts of the Droneboard nostr account (npub13twdjamspmt5gzm3kczydmntnw5mxp8dr788ucxnqy40gvl3gm2qxwypc9), fetched client-side from relays.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Released (0.1.7, beta): swipe in Slovak and English at once; neural next-word prediction (SK, EN) mixed with trigram statistics; offline dictation; Gboard-like look and defaults; emoji search by any part of a word and emoji suggestions; clipboard history, split and one-handed layouts, themes (from HeliBoard).
- In progress, must be labelled as coming, never as available: Czech, then Spanish and German; resizable / compact keyboard for tablets and foldables.
- Swipe accuracy evidence: on the offline Czech "sink-or-swipe" gesture set, Google's gesture library 82.4 % top-1 / 96.3 % top-3 vs Droneboard 81.8 % / 95.8 %. Present as a direction, not a promise.
- Required attribution: "Swipe typing powered by FUTO Swipe". Fork of HeliBoard (GPL-3.0). Bluetooth recording code from Third Ear.
- Stack: existing static generator (website/build.py, stdlib Python) producing plain HTML/CSS/JS; external scripts only pinned from a CDN. No trackers, no analytics, no external fonts that phone home (self-host fonts).

## Product Principles

1. Nothing leaves the phone — say it plainly and prove it (no INTERNET permission).
2. Feels like what you already use: Gboard habits keep working.
3. Honest beta: show what works today, label what is coming.
4. Open and free: GPL-3.0, credit the projects it stands on.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Five languages with natural (not translated-sounding) copy; WCAG AA contrast; works without JavaScript except the nostr feed.
