dictation that stays on the phone.

Droneboard types what you say with the help of Offline Speech to Text, a free app that turns speech into text on your phone. Nothing goes online.

offline speech to text

Offline Speech to Text is a free, open-source Android app. It runs speech recognition models on the phone itself and doesn’t need internet. Droneboard has no speech engine of its own; it hands the audio to this app.

how it works

  1. Tap the mic at the right end of the suggestion strip and start talking. Tap it again when you’re done.
  2. The keyboard records the audio itself. With a Bluetooth headset, LE Audio earbuds or hearing aids connected, it uses their microphone, and if the headset stays silent it switches to the phone’s microphone.
  3. Long dictation is split into pieces under a minute each, so you can keep talking past the one-minute mark.
  4. Offline Speech to Text transcribes each piece on the phone, and the text appears in the field you were typing in.

The audio never leaves the phone. Droneboard has no INTERNET permission, and Offline Speech to Text transcribes on the device.

set it up

  1. Install Offline Speech to Text from Zapstore.
  2. Open it once and download a speech model.
  3. Switch to Droneboard, tap the mic in the suggestion strip and talk.
Offline Speech to Text on Zapstore

if you speak several languages

In Offline Speech to Text, choose the model Cadence V2, set the language to auto-detect, and in the list of languages you speak, keep only the ones you want to dictate in.

The default model, Parakeet, also detects the language on its own, but you can’t limit it. It may take Slovak for Czech or Polish and then keep writing in that language. Cadence V2 only picks from the languages you chose.
