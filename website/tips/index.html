things you may not know it does.

Droneboard has gestures and settings that are easy to miss. Each tip says whether it works out of the box or needs switching on, and where. “Settings” are Droneboard’s own settings: tap the arrow at the left end of the suggestion strip to open the toolbar, then the gear.

text and cursor

  1. 01

    slide along the space bar to move the cursor

    Put a finger on the space bar and slide left or right: the cursor follows. It works out of the box. What the slide does can be changed in Settings → Advanced → Horizontal spacebar swipe gesture.

  2. 02

    hold the space bar for a trackpad

    Press and hold the space bar, then slide anywhere on the keyboard to steer the cursor like on a laptop trackpad. Lifting your finger does not type a space. It is on by default; Settings → Advanced → Long press on space bar offers Trackpad, Switch language or keyboard, or None.

  3. 03

    slide left from the delete key to select and delete

    Hold the delete key and slide left: the text before the cursor is selected word by word, and lifting your finger deletes it. Slide back to the right to select less. Settings → Advanced → Delete swipe switches between Words (the default) and Letters.

typing

  1. 04

    long-press for accents and symbols

    Hold a key to get more characters, such as accented letters and symbols, and slide to the one you want. The small characters in the corner of a key show what waits under a long press. They are on by default: Settings → Preferences → Show key hints.

  2. 05

    swipe over the letters

    Slide through the letters of a word without lifting your finger, then lift. Droneboard types the word and adds the space. It is on by default (Settings → Gesture Typing). Swipe search width on the same screen trades speed for accuracy; 200 is the recommended value.

  3. 06

    mix languages without switching

    This needs a one-time setup. In Settings → Languages & Layouts open a language and pick more languages under Multilingual typing. Suggestions and swipes then handle words from all of them, with no language switch in between.

  4. 07

    slovak and czech without diacritics

    If you type “dakujem” instead of “ďakujem”, add Slovak (no diacritics) or Czech (no diacritics) in Settings → Languages & Layouts. They are separate languages with their own dictionaries, so suggestions and swipes work without diacritics.

  5. 08

    number row

    In password fields the number row appears on its own. To get it in every field, switch on Settings → Preferences → Number row.

  6. 09

    search emoji while you type

    Type a colon and a word, for example :rocket, and matching emoji appear in the suggestion strip. The emoji panel also has a search key in its bottom row. Inline search is on by default: Settings → Text correction → Inline emoji search.

keyboard and toolbar

  1. 10

    size and position of the keyboard

    Tap Size & position in the toolbar and drag the edges or corners to resize. Choose Full width, Compact, One-handed, Floating or Split, and tap Done when it fits. A floating keyboard moves by its drag handle.

  2. 11

    make the toolbar yours

    The arrow at the left end of the suggestion strip opens the toolbar: Clipboard, D-pad (arrow keys), Undo, Redo and more. Settings → Toolbar → Select toolbar keys changes the keys and their order. The keys that stay in the strip (the mic, by default) are chosen under Select pinned toolbar keys.

  3. 12

    clipboard history

    Tap Clipboard in the toolbar to paste something you copied a while ago. Entries are kept for 10 minutes by default; press and hold one to pin it, and it stays. Settings are in Settings → Preferences → Clipboard history.

voice and privacy

  1. 13

    dictate with the mic

    The mic at the right end of the suggestion strip types what you say. It needs the free app Offline Speech to Text, installed separately, and works without internet. How dictation works.

  2. 14

    incognito mode

    Switch on Settings → Advanced → Force incognito mode and Droneboard stops learning new words. It is off by default. You can also add the same key to the toolbar.
