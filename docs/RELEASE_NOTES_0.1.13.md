- New languages for typing without háčky and čárky: "Slovak (no diacritics)" and "Czech (no diacritics)". Each one has its own dictionaries, so suggestions, swipe and the spell checker give words without accents ("dakujem", "uz", "muze"). Turn them on in Languages & Layouts. They also work together with other languages, for example with English.
- Slovak without diacritics has its own neural next-word model.
- New neural models for Slovak, Czech and English. The English model is smaller.

Beta. Dictation needs Offline Speech to Text from Zapstore.
