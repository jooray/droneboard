First public beta.

- Swipe typing in Slovak and English at once, powered by FUTO Swipe with Droneboard's own decoder. It learns the words you type.
- Next-word predictions from a small neural network running on the phone (Slovak, English), mixed with word-triple statistics.
- Dictation with Offline Speech to Text, recording from the phone or Bluetooth, LE Audio and hearing-aid microphones.
- Gboard-like look and defaults, emoji search by any part of a word, emoji suggestions while typing.
- Works fully offline: no INTERNET permission.

Dictation needs Offline Speech to Text installed from Zapstore.
